# Production INPUT

Use this reference for a production dispatch of new prose, continuation, or revision. The main task sends one concise packet to one `style_editor`; it is a generation boundary, not a prose outline or laboratory record.

## Source resolution

Read the sources relevant to the request and keep their roles separate in the packet:

- the exact requested result and the author's exact delta;
- scenario facts, fixed events in chronological order, and the current source scene where applicable;
- the selected project's populated glossary and relevant ability descriptions, including term meanings, effects and limits. Resolve the author -> series -> book binding and follow the series entry point: its glossary may be distributed across linked system, world, role and combat notes rather than a single file. An empty generic glossary template does not establish that the series glossary is missing; a class summary alone does not establish an ability's exact effect;
- character knowledge at the moment and reader access to each consequential fact;
- viewpoint, current intention and stake, intended reader effect, protected beats, presentation constraints, and prohibited additions;
- applicable confirmed author, genre, narrative, and correctness rules, plus any production-allowed source excerpts with their provenance.
- the selected series' linked lexical dictionary (`04 Анализ/Лексический словарь.md`): independent author-approved words to avoid and words to prefer, with scope and context. Pass relevant entries, not substitution pairs. Preference never requires insertion, overrides meaning, or excuses nearby repetition; retain exact canonical terms and source quotations. Do not import another series' lists or invent entries when this dictionary is empty.
- Load only the locally selected author's confirmed private profile and scene-relevant excerpts with provenance from their own vault. Never bundle or transfer private profiles or examples to another project.

`source_resolution` is complete only when every relevant source has been read and any truncation, missing source, conflict, or ambiguity has been resolved. Use only confirmed, applicable voice material. Do not promote an unselected profile or a laboratory hypothesis into the packet. Existing prose excerpts may be used in production when their provenance is stated; samples reserved for blind training remain withheld from candidate writers.

## Story time and world behavior

For a scene in an established project, resolve the requested story point rather than freezing the character at the end of the earliest manuscript fragment. An absent role, skill, or item in that fragment is missing evidence about later events, not a prohibition. Read applicable later author decisions and linked project discussions when available; a direct author correction governs the corrected fact. Keep its provenance and distinguish character planning from a demonstrated in-story acquisition.

The concise INPUT includes the scene-relevant world mechanism: the character's role, how that role acquires information and affects the world, available abilities and their limits at this point, and the behavior of a named professional opponent. Separate confirmed mechanisms, unresolved progression, and proposed scene details. Do not substitute an unrelated starter class or invent replacement skills merely because exact unlock levels are missing. If a consequential ability choice remains unresolved after source lookup, ask the narrow question rather than changing the character's role.

In worlds that reinterpret real professions, terminology must govern actions and consequences. A label or costume alone does not establish the profession. Give the writer the profession's relevant function and a grounded manifestation; leave unconfirmed manifestations as proposals. A fantasy presentation can coexist with professional logic, without either arbitrary renamed spells or a literal contemporary workplace. Not every monster needs a profession.

When a quick reviewer is used, provide the relevant author decisions and source evidence alongside the generated INPUT. A consistent passage can still follow a mistaken INPUT; the reviewer should distinguish those failures. Check whether the scene's decisive information and actions follow the actual role and world mechanism, separately from voice and local physical continuity. This adds no mandatory reviewer, pre-prose approval stage, or extra generation.

## Facts and presentation

Scenario facts constrain what happens and fixed events retain their chronological order. They do not prescribe sentence rhythm, an object inventory, or the order in which the reader encounters details. Respect the author's explicit presentation constraints and protected beats. Introduce a significant object when its perceptual or causal function activates, unless the author explicitly requires earlier reader access.

## Writer boundary

The writer silently builds the necessary causal and perceptual structure, then returns finished prose in the same response. Production requires no JSON, ledger, hash, generation record, or pre-prose gate.

Apply detailed knowledge, physical, timing, boundary, and material-operation checks where a foregrounded event carries consequential risk. Do not turn ordinary movement into a recipe when no ambiguity or consequence depends on it.

Before delivery, self-check scenario preservation independently from voice, sentence rhythm, and motivated attention. Both checks must pass: the prose keeps the resolved event, knowledge, and protected-beat boundary, and it preserves the applicable voice and selects each foreground detail for a present perceptual or causal reason.
